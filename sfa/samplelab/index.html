Waveform

Click to add a cut. Drag a cut to move it. Double-click a cut to remove it. With a slice selected, [ ] nudge its start by 1 ms (shift: 10 ms) and Del joins it to the one before. Ctrl+Z undoes. Wheel zooms, shift + wheel pans. Click a slice in the table or press 1-9 0 Q-Y to hear it.

Auto-chop

Slices

The bank holds up to 16 samples and about 4.2 s of audio in all, mono, 22.05 kHz, 16 bit. Every sample is normalised to -1 dB by the packer, so relative levels between slices are not kept. Fades of 1.5 ms in and 6 ms out stop clicks at the cut.

Export

or send to

The zip holds NN_NAME.wav files (22.05 kHz, mono, 16 bit) and kit.json. To put them on a unit: unzip into a folder, run tools/sfa pack FOLDER ecb (or as), then flash with tools/sfa up ecb. Open this page from the SFA Console to send a kit straight to a unit's bank.
