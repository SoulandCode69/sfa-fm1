Firmware for the FM-1

Two complete sound-and-sequencer firmwares, tested in a simulator before they touch a unit, with every sample original and CC0.

Firmware

Earth Creature Beats

Five engines in animal form: Otter (polysynth), Cat (drums), Fox (808 and bass), Hopper (sampler) and Beaver.

  • Presets, kits and a sample bank
  • Clean output: distortion is treated as a bug
  • Download: coming soon
Firmware

Alien Stack

The second voice of the same platform: Mantis drums, Reptiles bass, Nordics, Greys and Ship.

  • Eight drum kits, a 24-preset Greys voice, a song builder
  • Measured against Earth Creature Beats for loudness and tone
  • Download: coming soon

Free tools

They run in the browser, with no account and nothing uploaded. Your audio stays on your machine.

Tool

SFA Analyzer

Spectrum, spectrogram, loudness (BS.1770), true peak, stereo width and a click finder. Compare two files side by side, level-matched.

Tool

SFA Sample Lab

Drop a loop, chop it by transients, grid or silence, hear it as the unit will play it, export a kit.

Console

SFA Console

Simulate, render, measure and flash one unit at a time, plus planning pages for the next box. Runs on your own computer.

The box

A small groovebox with velocity pads, a proper screen, stereo audio, real MIDI and a memory card for samples. Target street price $150 to $250. It is a plan, not a product yet.

What we are aiming for

  • 16 velocity pads, 4 encoders
  • 48 kHz stereo audio, headphones
  • MIDI in and out, USB-C
  • Samples on microSD: minutes, not seconds

How it will work

  • The same engines as the FM-1 firmwares
  • Presets, kits and patterns as plain files
  • Copy a file to share a sound; no rebuild

Where it stands

  • Specs and parts list drafted
  • Architecture designed
  • Next: an engine running on a development board
