# Hardware

Our own small groovebox: a plan, not a product.

* `prototype/direction.json`: the form-factor brief (2026-11-10): keys, pads, screen, I/O, pipeline, open questions.
* `prototype/parts-research.json` and `PARTS-SOURCES.md`: what a parts search found, with the pages opened.
* `prototype/spec.json`: research (specs, platforms, costed parts list, improvements, open questions), with `README.md` and `SOURCES.md`.
* `prototype/compare.json`: the comparison board (what was checked, what is a goal, what is unknown).
* `prototype/plan.json`: our decisions, edited in the console (Planner, Decisions, Engine map, Presets). Created on first edit.

Prices in the parts list are mostly estimates; the console marks which are quotes. Read `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` for how the
engines reach this hardware and what that implies for the form factor.
