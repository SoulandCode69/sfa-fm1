# SFA PROTO

The test bed for Sin Fetish Audio's own engines on the FM-1: Alien Stack's body (Nordics, Mantis, Reptiles, the Ship,
the mixer with sends, reverb and master) with **SFA ONE** (core/sfa_one.c) in the fourth slot. Flash it on a unit to
play the new engine on hardware (`tools/go.sh proto`, identity FM-1_5900, pages at http://localhost:8579/), read its
cost off the mixer page's CPU %, then flash Alien Stack back (`tools/go.sh visitors`) whenever you like: one firmware
per unit, any order.

SFA ONE's pages here: MACROS (TONE BODY COLOR MOTION), EXCITER, RESONATOR, FILTER, MOTION; ALGO browses its presets
(First Light, Pluck, Bowed, Glass Bar, Breath Pipe, SFA Bass, Folded Keys, Chord Cloud, Strike, Singing, Deep Bell,
Reed, Tension Pad, Kalimba One, Wire, Sub One).
