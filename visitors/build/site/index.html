ALIEN STACK

Alien Stack is a second firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1 from the people behind Earth Creature Beats: a synthesized drum machine, an alien bass bank, a polysynth, a sampler that samples itself, and a ship that sequences them all, with scenes and a song, parameter locks on every step, polymeter, and a reverb bus. Same hardware, another world.

Version dev · for the FM-1 · built on fm1-base · all audio original, CC0

NORDICS Poly synth

A 4-voice polysynth of pads, choirs, leads and arps.

MANTIS Drum synth

Sixteen synthesized drum voices, eight kits, every voice shaped per pad. No samples.

REPTILES Bass & lead

Sixteen chromatic alien one-shots: growls, folded subs, lasers, vowels; plus 24 synth basses.

GREYS Sampler

Sample the output or load your own; chop by transients; play the slices. Drone, transmission, sweep and choir on board.

SHIP Sequencer

8 patterns, scenes, a 16-slot song, ratchets, probability, groove generators, step entry and live record.
